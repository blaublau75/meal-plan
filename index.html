Plan guide & tips
🧊
Always cook fresh
Histamine builds in food as it ages. Cook and eat each meal the same day. If prepping ahead, freeze same-day portions immediately — don't refrigerate protein overnight.
🌱
The flaxseed rule
Always grind flaxseed immediately before use. Pre-ground or stored ground flaxseed oxidises rapidly and can trigger MCAS reactions. Buy whole flaxseeds and grind in a small spice or coffee grinder right before eating. Takes 10 seconds.
🌿
Seeds at a glance
Chia seeds — omega-3 ALA, fibre; add to porridge, eggs, or batters. Can be pre-measured.

Milled flaxseed — highest plant omega-3 source; grind fresh each time, stir into hot food immediately.

Pumpkin seeds — richest food source of magnesium; scatter over bowls, stir into rice or mash. Buy raw, unsalted.

Hemp seeds — omega-3 ALA + complete protein; mild flavour, sprinkle over anything.
🫒
EVOO is your main fat
Extra virgin olive oil is used throughout this plan. Aim for at least 2 tablespoons per day — it is one of the most evidence-backed cardiovascular foods available. Use it for cooking, roasting, and as a finishing drizzle.
🧄
Garlic tip
Crush or chop garlic and wait 10 minutes before cooking. This activates allicin — the compound responsible for garlic's LDL-lowering and blood pressure benefits. Already chopped garlic that goes straight into a hot pan loses most of this benefit.
📅
Meal structure
Breakfast — optional lighter start. Great for days when you wake hungry or want to spread intake.

Meal 1 (~10–11 AM) — main protein-anchored meal. Stabilises blood sugar for the day.

Meal 2 (~5–6 PM) — evening meal.

Snack — low-histamine, heart-healthy. Eat between meals if energy dips.
💛
Turmeric + black pepper
Always pair turmeric with a pinch of black pepper. Piperine in black pepper increases curcumin absorption by up to 2000%. This combination is already in most recipes but keep it in mind when adding turmeric elsewhere.
⚠️
MCAS food safety
Avoid: aged/fermented foods, vinegar-based dressings, smoked fish, canned fish, alcohol, tomatoes, strawberries, citrus juice, chocolate, leftover protein.

Trial cautiously: avocado, spinach, eggplant, pre-packaged walnuts.

Safe: freshly cooked chicken/turkey/fish, fresh eggs, all vegetables in this plan, fresh low-sugar fruit, oats, legumes cooked same-day.
❤️
About Lp(a)
Lp(a) is ~90% genetically determined and responds minimally to diet alone. Diet here is about reducing overall cardiovascular risk — cutting saturated fat, boosting soluble fibre, and maximising omega-3. If your Lp(a) is significantly elevated (>50 mg/dL), discuss PCSK9 inhibitors or RNA-targeted therapies with your cardiologist.
